Two keynotes (HER CHAIR; his headset's hook) and two webs (F2 fires here; her doubling is staged here) therefore dress an ABSENCE. Ruling needed before P2 of 3.18 and 3.19: a second placement/pose per character in these rooms (a C2 asset question), or the rooms dress the chair and the hook as biography of someone who is elsewhere (this brief's default: dress the absence).
- **0-O · THE PANEL'S LABELS ARE THE MAP, AND THE MAP IS UNWRITTEN.** The lighting bible makes the cascade "authored two-stage darkness with real circuit GEOGRAPHY (C's zone dies, then B's; restoring in order relights in order; the panel's labels are the map)" [restoration-lighting-bible.md §STATES CASCADE]. The stage end of the building drops dark" [Data/GameText.csv 52] and circuit F = the transmitter, "the marshal's tie" [GameText 55, 539; restoration-props-packet.md D09]. The reference assigns no room to any circuit (§0.2 The cascade, as built); the reaction matrix adds a per-casualty corridor circuit (B-R2) and the room inventory a kitchen sub-panel [restoration-reaction-matrix.md THE BUILDING; restoration-room-inventory.md §4]. Every CASCADE row in briefs 3.1–3.20 is OPEN for the same reason, and C18 (Lighting.csv) cannot fill a circuit column without it.
